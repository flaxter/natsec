🏡 Ten Good Years

You're in charge of a small country's budget. Can you get it through ten years in one piece?

  1. Each year you get $100m. Split it between factories and schools for girls using the slider next to the board.
  2. Press Spend it. Your town grows. Some years a shock hits: conflict, unrest, storms, outbreaks.
  3. Watch the four meters. Money back, peace, trust, and what gets paid forward to the next generation.
